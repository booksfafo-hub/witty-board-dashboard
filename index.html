What this does not claim

  • Opportunity outcomes can be correlated; the range includes an explicit portfolio-risk buffer.
  • Pipeline is not signed backlog, recognized revenue, or cash.
  • Any material change in close dates, values, or evidence requires a forecast refresh.
  • Historical win rates are evidence, not a substitute for deal-specific judgment.

Validation gates

  1. Out-of-sample value error within an agreed tolerance.
  2. Bias review by region, sector, and deal size.
  3. Conversion and time-to-close assumptions documented separately.
  4. Commercial owner signs off on capacity and investment needs.

Decision rule

Keep the financial plan clean

Potential lead generation can inform sales capacity choices. It becomes forecast only after an opportunity exists, is qualified, and is assessed through the same governed probability process as every other deal.
